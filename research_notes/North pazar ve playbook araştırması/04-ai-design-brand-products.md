## Key question 1: Product teardown (what each does, feature inventory, request-to-outcome flow, export/hand-off, pricing, agentic vs assistive)

### Takeaway
The market has split into three layers: (a) agentic generators that take a brief and return a whole artifact with a built-in brand/design-system contract (Claude Design, Figma Make, Framer Agent, Webflow AI Site Builder, Relume, Higgsfield Ads Studio, Adobe Firefly "brand kit" skill, Open Design); (b) assistive editors where AI fills a canvas the human still drives (Canva, Adobe Express, Uizard, Wix/Tailor/Looka/Brandmark logo wizards, Durable); (c) model/reference utilities with no workflow of their own (Recraft, Midjourney, Ideogram, Lummi, Mobbin, Unicorn Studio, Spline). Every agentic product converges on the same four moves: structured intake -> a brand/design-system file as a contract -> N variants -> anchored comments that produce a new version -> packaged hand-off to code.

### Inferences
- Agentic vs assistive: Claude Design, Figma Make, Framer Agent, Webflow AI, Relume, Stitch, Higgsfield Ads Studio/brand-asset-creation, Pencil, AdCreative, Adobe Firefly skills and Open Design are agentic; Canva, Adobe Express, Uizard, Durable and all logo wizards (Looka, Brandmark, Tailor, Wix) are assistive; Recraft, Midjourney, Ideogram, Lummi, Mobbin, Spline, Unicorn are utilities North would call, not compete with.
- The logo wizards sell files and templates, not design judgement: their intake is name + industry + style/colour chips, and their consistency comes from re-skinning templates with the chosen logo. North's brand-kit must beat this on (1) real vector files and (2) a machine-readable kit, not on template count.
- The two most-liked review mechanics in 2026 are (a) sliders/knobs the agent invents per design (Claude Design, Open Design tweaks panel) and (b) comments or annotations anchored to a preview element that become the next prompt (Claude Design, Figma Make, Lovable, v0). Both keep the founder out of a design tool.
- Model swapping inside one tool produces visibly different directions; North can get cheap diversity by varying taste dials and model/prompt per direction rather than relying on one call.
- Credit exhaustion is the top complaint about Claude Design; North's per-task budgets and "refine or pivot" stop rules are a differentiator if surfaced in the UI.

## Key question 2: How the best products represent a brand kit as data, and what the brand questionnaire looks like

### Takeaway
Three data formats dominate: a Markdown contract read by agents (Stitch/VoltAgent `DESIGN.md` with 9 sections; Figma `Guidelines.md`; Framer DESIGN.md references; Open Design packages adding `manifest.json` + `tokens.css`), a UI brand record (Canva/Adobe/GoDaddy/Klaviyo: logos, colours, fonts, voice), and a prompt-level lock (Higgsfield "Brand Lock" JSON repeated verbatim in every generation; Recraft saved Style + palette; Midjourney profile + `--sref`). Questionnaires have shrunk to 4-15 questions, with the strongest tools extracting most answers from a URL or codebase and asking only for gaps.

### Cited Findings
- DESIGN.md (Stitch format, as catalogued by VoltAgent): 1 Visual Theme & Atmosphere, 2 Color Palette & Roles (semantic name, hex, role), 3 Typography Rules (families + hierarchy table), 4 Component Stylings (buttons, cards, inputs, nav, states), 5 Layout Principles (spacing scale, grid), 6 Depth & Elevation, 7 Do's and Don'ts, 8 Responsive Behavior (breakpoints, touch targets), 9 Agent Prompt Guide; plain Markdown, "no Figma exports, JSON schemas, or special tooling" — [VoltAgent/awesome-design-md](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md)
- Open Design: "Each render reads the active design-system package's DESIGN.md as its core brand contract"; newer packages add `manifest.json`, `tokens.css`, components, assets, provenance; users with a brand "supply a screenshot or URL, and the agent codifies a reusable DESIGN.md" — [GitHub](https://github.com/nexu-io/open-design)
- Figma Make: `Guidelines.md` with "general rules" and "design system rules, including component sections with usage and variants"; guidelines can be uploaded, written, or generated by analysing npm packages ("components, styles, tokens, and general rules"); Make Kits link components to npm packages so "the design system [is] the instruction set for AI generation"; attachments: PDF, MD, CSV, JSON, screenshots, SVG, images — [Figma help](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/33665861260823), [creativeainews](https://www.creativeainews.com/blog/figma-make-kits-attachments-ai-design/); users still ask for org-wide central guidelines (forum, March 2026) — [Figma forum](https://forum.figma.com/suggest-a-feature-11/figma-make-centralized-context-guidelines-for-organization-51450)
- Claude Design: design system built "from your codebase and design files"; "projects automatically use your colors, typography, and components"; dedicated UI "to store, review, and make changes to them" — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-design-anthropic-labs), [Flux Academy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fRAUuluAirM); criticised as "its own design system format ... not standardized" — [BGP](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6OpWU-HrsuQ)

### Inferences

- The questionnaire should be gap-driven: pre-fill from the company memory (onboarding already has name/offer/audience), ask at most 4-6 questions, add three taste dials, and separate "official assets" from "inspiration" uploads.
- "Avoid" rules (at least two) and "forbidden colours" belong in the kit; they are what stops drift in later ad/social outputs.
- A per-format `applications` override block (carousel vs poster vs OG image) prevents the kit from being either too rigid or ignored.

### Gaps
- Canva's and Adobe's exact brand-kit schemas (voice fields, do/don't) are behind 403 pages; Pencil's brand setup is undocumented in retrieved sources.

## Key question 3: Best-practice iterative workflow (drafts -> critique -> variants -> revision) and how critique is encoded

### Takeaway
The agency pattern (brief -> 2-3 concepts -> pick -> rounds of anchored feedback) is now encoded in products as staged approvals with exactly three candidates per stage, one approval per slot, and dependency invalidation when an upstream slot changes. Critique is encoded three ways: NN/g's goal-anchored conversational rules, A List Apart's observation-impact-request comment template with severity markers, and tool rubrics (Impeccable: Nielsen 0-4 scoring, P0-P3 severity, "design specificity" verdict, persona red flags; Refactoring UI's concrete rules as a checklist; a growing "AI-slop" tell list).

### Cited Findings
- NN/g: a critique "analyzes a design and gives feedback on whether it meets its objectives ... not to judge it"; roles presenter / critiquer / facilitator / recorder; "clear scope", "shared objectives", "conversation over command"; facilitator reframes directives into questions ("How does this layout make it easier for the user to accomplish their task?"); methods: round robin or quotas of positive and negative comments; pitfalls: no agreed personas/objectives, rushing to solve, only negatives — [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/design-critiques/)
- A List Apart async critique: each comment = Observation (from a named perspective) + Impact + Question/request; severity markers Blocking / Should change / Positive / Question; "one comment per topic so each discussion maps to a single task"; anchor with screenshots; keep resolved discussions visible; template "[Marker] Area — Context. Observation from [perspective]. Impact. Question/request?" — [A List Apart](https://alistapart.com/article/async-design-critique-giving-feedback/)
- Impeccable `critique`: two isolated assessments (A design review: specificity first, then hierarchy, IA, emotional fit, typography, colour, a11y, states, copy, cognitive load, Nielsen heuristics, persona red flags; B evidence: deterministic detector on markup/live page), synthesised; Nielsen's 10 heuristics scored 0-4 (bands 36-40 Excellent ... 0-11 Critical), 8-item cognitive-load checklist, working-memory rule (<=4 options good, >=8 overloaded), severity P0-P3 with tie-breaker "would a user contact support?", "Design Specificity verdict: whether the interface could be used unchanged by an unrelated product", output = score table, what's working (2-3), priority issues (3-5 with severity/what/why/fix), questions; "Inline execution is degraded and must be labeled" — [impeccable critique.md](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/main/skill/reference/critique.md)
- Refactoring UI rules usable as a checklist: "size alone doesn't create hierarchy", "avoid grey text on colored backgrounds", "start with generous whitespace, then tighten", "define a spacing and sizing system", "set a type scale", "keep line length in check", "align text by baseline", "use HSL", "define shades up front", "greys can carry a hue", "emulate a consistent light source", "shadows work better in two parts", "customize framework defaults", "use fewer borders"; core message "Design with tactics, not talent"; $99-$149 — [refactoringui.com](https://www.refactoringui.com/)
- AI-slop tells (noqta, 2026-06-04): oversized border radius everywhere, random purple-to-blue glow gradients, excessive letter spacing on uppercase labels, fake "Live/New" badges, thick one-side coloured card border; fixes: design tokens instead of adjectives, a Markdown design-system doc given as context, distinctive display + clean body font, real screenshots/data, specific copy — [noqta](https://noqta.tn/en/blog/ai-design-slop-overused-ui-patterns-fix-2026); the Anthropic harness evaluator scored design quality, originality, craft, functionality and LLM evaluators "tend to be generous" (see dept doc)
- Growth.Design critiques are organised by psychology principle (Labor Perception Bias, Zeigarnik Effect, Framing Effect, Social Proof, Mental Models, Scarcity, JTBD) and category (Onboarding, Retention, Revenue, Referral) — [Growth.Design](https://growth.design/case-studies)
- Staged approval encoded as product rules (Higgsfield `concept-boards.md`, `qa-and-iteration.md`): palette (2-3 options with hex, bg/text colours, rationale, usage examples, 2-3 logo mechanism seeds) -> user picks -> "exactly three original logo mechanisms" generated with identical model/palette/background/quality parameters -> pick -> 2-3 type pairs shown next to the chosen mark -> pick; "Never infer approval from silence or tool success"; "Assume the first pass contains inconsistencies"; set-level consistency matrix `Asset | Logo | Palette | Type | Grid/spacing | Shape/device | Format | Status`; "Do not accept a vision model's statement as proof of exact hex, font, spacing, or radius" (deterministic checks for dimensions, colours, fonts, positions); repair = "name the failed Brand Lock rule ... regenerate only the failing asset"; reject "generic marble/pedestal luxury scenes, arbitrary gradients, plastic sheen, excessive bloom, floating objects, meaningless props, and fake copy"; stable file names so the user can say "revise carousel card 3" — Higgsfield MCP bundle
- Claude Design loop: "Claude builds the first version, and then you refine through conversation, inline comments, direct edits, or custom sliders" — [Flux Academy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fRAUuluAirM); Figma Make: queued, reorderable prompts and "clear chat context" on drift — [Figma help](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/31304485164695); Framer: branches for agent edits, "Start a new [conversation] for each task to keep the agent focused" — [framer.com/ai](https://www.framer.com/ai/); Open Design: live todo card "for mid-task redirects" — [GitHub](https://github.com/nexu-io/open-design)
- Variant counts: Stitch default 3, Kombai 1-3 + passes, Framer hero "several", Higgsfield logos exactly 3 / palettes 2-3 / type 2-3, Magic Media 4 per prompt (dept doc; [fast.io](https://fast.io/resources/canva-ai-review-2026/))

### Inferences
- North's critic should combine the three encodings: deterministic gates (already in the dept doc) + a Nielsen/Refactoring-UI/slop-tell rubric scored with evidence crops + a "design specificity" yes/no that fails anything usable by an unrelated product. Scores calibrated with few-shot examples as the Anthropic harness post advises.
- Critique output for the founder should use the A List Apart shape (marker, area, observation, impact, request), one finding per comment, since those map one-to-one to `revise_artifact` work items.
- Staged approvals with dependency invalidation (change palette -> logo candidates regenerate; change logo -> brandbook pages invalidated) should be modelled as data on `BrandKit` slots, not as prompt text.
- Keep "exactly 3" as the default candidate count per stage; it is the industry norm and matches the founder's "pick among variants" UX.

### Gaps
- NN/g's paid critique cheat-sheet and Growth.Design's full scoring are not public; the open-source design-critiquer skills found exclude brand strategy.
- No controlled study found comparing 2 vs 3 vs 5 variants for non-designer decision quality.

## Key question 4: How products expose design to non-designers for review (side-by-side, annotated comments, version history, "pick one")

### Takeaway
Non-designer review in 2026 means: a preview you can click, a small set of named options to pick from, comments pinned to a spot that turn into the next version, sliders for taste without a design tool, and a quote before spend; version branching is still weak everywhere except Framer branches and v0's apply/diff/revert.

### Cited Findings
- Claude Design: inline comments on elements, direct text editing, sketch annotations, auto-generated knobs; "a surprisingly hands-on loop for a tool that starts from a text prompt"; "you don't have a fully interactive visual canvas where you can just drag and drop" — [Flux Academy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fRAUuluAirM); org-scoped sharing (private, view-by-link, edit) — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-design-anthropic-labs)
- Figma Make: select element + properties panel, annotations on the preview, share preview, publish prototype; copy to Figma canvas prompts "does it look good?" — [Figma help](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/31304485164695), [Vika Taylor](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X81GJz2Z8IA); branching/iteration history still requested by users (dept doc forum link)
- Framer: hero variations "compare the alternatives, and drop your preferred one onto the site"; branches isolate agent edits — [createwith](https://www.createwith.com/tool/framer/updates/framer-adds-hero-variations-to-framer-agent), [framer.com/ai](https://www.framer.com/ai/)
- Higgsfield: palette/type options rendered as editable HTML boards shown inline as PNG with HTML download; "Reply with the option you prefer and any colors you want changed, added, or removed"; "Do not use a structured question tool or a multiple-choice list" for the pick; logo review shows three SVGs with direct links; brandbook delivered as "two file links plus the font-install warning only"; Ads Studio shows credits before generation and "Generation starts only on an explicit yes to that price" — MCP bundle; research progress card shows five stages — [Higgsfield blog](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/higgsfield-ads-studio)
- Bloom onboarding: confirm logo ("Does the logo look accurate? Upload Another / Looks Good"), pick 3 asset types, pick 3 inspiring ads, then a first-batch modal with 5 more locked thumbnails and "Download / Continue to workspace" — [Mobbin Bloom](https://mobbin.com/flows/b6097b62-f994-4928-9780-ef30fd006872)
- Open Design: direction picker with 5 curated directions, live todo card, tweaks panel re-renders the iframe "without a reload" — [GitHub](https://github.com/nexu-io/open-design)
- Steve Builds Websites: editing by prompt "is a terrible way of editing websites. Turns out drag and drop is just faster"; "all these AI tools charge you based on how many prompts you write ... even when the prompts break your website" — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wbyPMZ6Y6Qw)
- Uizard markets a "design reviews" AI feature with per-plan limits — [uizard.io/pricing](https://uizard.io/pricing/); AdCreative and Pencil attach a numeric score to each creative (Conversion Score /100; Media Performance Score 0-100) so non-designers can rank — [Semrush KB](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1424-adcreative-ai), [vidmuse](https://vidmuse.ai/blog/pencil-ai-review)
- Lovable/v0 mechanics (select/draw/comment modes; pending edits -> Apply -> new version with diff/revert) are in the dept doc.

### Inferences
- For North's founder (who "only answers decision cards"), the review surface should be: named directions side by side at 390/1440 with a one-click pick; pinned comments with severity chips that batch into one revision; knobs for the 3-4 taste parameters the agent exposes per artifact; a cost quote on every regenerate; and a version list with diff and revert. Keep drafts in a review queue, not in the decision-card quota.
- Direct manipulation for trivial edits (swap an image, change a word) must not round-trip through the agent; expose text and image slots as editable fields on the artifact.
- Score chips (critic score, on-brand score) next to each variant help a non-designer pick without forcing a choice; Autopilot can pick the top score if the founder opts in.

### Gaps
- No usability study of non-designers picking among AI variants was found; evidence is product behaviour and reviewer opinion.
- Figma Make's comment threads and version-restore details were not in the retrieved help pages.

## Key question 5: Which features North should adopt (ranked) and which patterns to avoid

### Takeaway
Adopt the contract-first, staged, three-candidate, comment-to-version loop with a machine-readable brand kit and a hard critic; avoid prompt-only editing, template re-skinning, unquoted spend, generous self-grading and proprietary kit formats.

### Cited Findings
- All evidence is cited in KQ1-KQ4 above; this section ranks it.

### Inferences
Ranked features North should adopt:
1. Brand kit as data with lock states: Zod `BrandKit` JSON (Higgsfield Brand Lock fields + Stitch DESIGN.md 9 sections), per-field `fixed/proposed/unknown` and evidence labels, >=2 avoid rules, per-format `applications`; render to DESIGN.md + tokens.json for every later persona (Open Design, Stitch, Framer all consume DESIGN.md).
2. Gap-driven intake: pre-fill from company memory, <=6 questions, three taste dials (Restrained-Expressive, Geometric-Organic, Familiar-Experimental), separate official-asset vs inspiration uploads, "confirm your logo" step (Bloom/Klaviyo pattern), URL extraction when a site exists (Higgsfield/AdCreative).
3. Staged approvals with exactly 3 candidates per slot (palette -> logo -> type -> directions), each pick persisted as an approval, dependency invalidation on change, candidates generated with identical parameters so the pick is about design, not noise.
4. Comment-to-version loop: comments anchored to a screenshot point/DOM selector, A List Apart shape (marker/observation/impact/request), batched into one `revise_artifact`, producing version N+1 with visual diff and revert (v0/Lovable/Claude Design/Figma Make).
5. Agent-generated knobs: 3-4 sliders per artifact (spacing, accent, density, hero layout) that re-render without a new agent run (Claude Design, Open Design tweaks panel); plus direct edit of text/image slots so trivial changes never cost a prompt (Steve Builds Websites complaint).
6. Critic with a "design specificity" fail: deterministic gates + Nielsen 0-4 + Refactoring UI rules + slop-tell list + "could an unrelated product use this unchanged?" (Impeccable); findings as P0-P3 with evidence crops; few-shot calibration.
7. Quote before spend and visible budget: show estimated cost per regenerate/batch and require a yes above a threshold (Higgsfield Ads Studio); surface remaining task budget to counter the "burns tokens" complaint about Claude Design.
8. Hand-off bundle: every direction ships as DESIGN.md + tokens + HTML + screenshots so the Frontend Engineer persona (and the founder's own Claude Code/Figma) can take it "with a single instruction" (Claude Design); offer Figma write-to-canvas and Canva export as optional connections.
9. Multi-angle creative batches: for ads/social, generate per selling angle (offer, ingredient, how-used, lifestyle) with the brand kit on, 10-20 per run, each with a critic/on-brand score, exportable in 1:1/9:16/16:9/3:4 (Higgsfield, AdCreative, Pencil).
10. Model/prompt diversity per direction: vary taste dials and prompt scaffolds (and optionally model) across the 3 directions, since identical prompts in one tool converge on the same layout and headline (Steve Builds Websites, Vika Taylor's model-swap test).
11. Reference libraries as optional connections: Mobbin MCP (Pro $10/mo) and Lummi for inspiration only, never as templates; Recraft Styles for illustration consistency; Midjourney profiles only if the founder connects their own account.
12. Reviewer-friendly delivery: stable variant names ("direction-b-v2"), two-link deliveries for files, font-install warnings, and no "beautiful"/"done" claims before QA (Higgsfield delivery contract).

Patterns to avoid:
- Prompt-only editing for small changes (30-60 s per edit, breaks pages, costs credits) — [Steve Builds Websites](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wbyPMZ6Y6Qw).
- Template re-skinning sold as identity (Looka/Tailor/Wix "heavily templated", Magic Design "converges on popular layouts"); North's kit must produce distinct directions, not one template in three colours.
- Vague-prompt generation with no context ("the output looks like AI made it") — [Flux Academy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fRAUuluAirM); never run a direction without a locked kit.
- Proprietary kit format with one-way hand-off (Claude Design critique: "not standardized", "can't design directly ... from Claude Code") — [BGP](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6OpWU-HrsuQ); use DESIGN.md/tokens so other tools can read it.
- Treating tool success, recency or the model's own opinion as approval; inferring approval from silence (Higgsfield rules); accepting a vision model's claim about exact hex/font/radius without a deterministic check.
- Unquoted or opaque credit burn (Claude Design limits, Lovable credits charged for broken edits).
- Fake success states: generated contact forms that send nowhere ([Steve Builds Websites](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wbyPMZ6Y6Qw)); North's evidence rule already forbids this.
- "AI-slop" visual defaults: uniform large radii, purple-blue glow gradients, tracked uppercase labels, fake badges, marble/pedestal mockups, Inter-everywhere ([noqta](https://noqta.tn/en/blog/ai-design-slop-overused-ui-patterns-fix-2026), Higgsfield QA list).
- Over-long guideline context: Figma warns more context "can confuse the LLM"; keep DESIGN.md compact and copy only the relevant lock block into prompts.
- Charging the founder's 3/day decision quota with design drafts; drafts belong in a separate review queue (North rule).
- Promising native Figma/Canva/PSD/AI/EPS files the pipeline cannot produce faithfully; promise SVG/PNG/PDF/HTML/PPTX and import caveats.

### Gaps
- Pricing for a North "design seat" vs. these tools cannot be benchmarked precisely because several vendor pages were unreachable; the cost model in the dept doc ($5.70-7.20 per full cycle) remains the reference.
- Whether founders prefer 2 or 3 directions, and whether Autopilot may auto-pick, remain open questions for the founder (dept doc section 5).
