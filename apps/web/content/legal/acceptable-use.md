# Acceptable use policy

This policy is part of the [terms of service](/legal/terms) between you and [operating entity]. Reports and questions go to [contact address]. The laws of [jurisdiction] apply.

## Platform rules

North's agents act inside the rules of the platforms they use, and so must you:

- **No spam.** Outreach is personal, to people with a plausible reason to hear from you, with a working way to opt out. Bulk unsolicited email is not allowed.
- **No fake accounts.** Agents never create or operate accounts that pretend to be someone else, and you may not ask them to.
- **No posting on the user's behalf.** Agents do not post, review or comment as you or anyone else on social networks, forums or review sites. Reddit and similar sources are read only.
- **Ads and email under platform policies.** Ad campaigns follow the ad platform's policies; email follows the mail provider's terms and anti-spam law.

## Not allowed

- Illegal products or services, or anything that infringes someone else's rights.
- Deceptive claims, impersonation or hidden pricing.
- Attempts to bypass the decision cards, the budgets, the kill switch or the authority checks.
- Using North to attack, probe or overload systems you do not own.

## Enforcement

We may pause agents or suspend an account that breaks this policy, and will tell you why at the email on your account.
