These terms are an agreement between you and [operating entity] ("North", "we") for the use of the North service. The laws of [jurisdiction] govern them. Questions go to [contact address].

## What North is

North is software that runs agents on your behalf for companies you create. You stay the decision maker: anything that touches the outside world with your money, your accounts or your name either follows a standing rule you accepted or waits for your answer on a decision card. You can stop every agent of a company at any time with the kill switch.

## Your account

- You must be old enough to enter a contract where you live and give accurate sign-up details.
- You are responsible for what happens under your account and for the accounts you connect (GitHub, Vercel, ad platforms, email, payments).
- You keep ownership of everything your companies produce: code (in your own GitHub), artifacts, data and the results recorded in the ledger.

## Plans, trial and credits

- A new account starts with a 14-day trial of the Solo plan without a card.
- Paid plans renew monthly through Stripe until you cancel in the billing portal. Prices and included credits are shown on the billing page before you pay.
- One credit is one agent run. Plan credits are granted each period and unused plan credits expire at the period end; credit packs never expire.
- North never moves your money from accounts it holds: spending (ads, domains, tools) happens only from your own connected accounts and within the budgets you set.

## When a subscription ends

Agents stop when the subscription or the trial ends. Your data is kept for 90 days, during which you can export it from Settings. After that it may be deleted.
